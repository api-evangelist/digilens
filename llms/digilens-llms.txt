## What DigiLens sells

- Crystal Waveguides — diffractive waveguide optical components made with a proprietary
  photopolymer material set, inkjet printing and holographic contact copy rather than
  etching. Supplied to partners including Samsung, Corning, Panasonic, Sony, Continental,
  Mitsubishi Chemical, UDC and Goertek for smartglasses, automotive AR head-up displays,
  smart helmets and avionics head-up guidance systems.
- ARGO — an all-in-one, rugged, standalone enterprise AR headset on the Qualcomm
  Snapdragon XR2 platform, Snapdragon Spaces Ready, certified for Microsoft Intune MDM.
- DigiOS — DigiLens' AOSP Android 12 operating system for ARGO, with voice UI, gaze and
  scroll-wheel interaction, projector control, OpenXR and WebXR support, and OTA updates.
- DigiSaaS — DigiLens' partner software ecosystem for ARGO (Manifest, Oculum, RemoteSpark,
  Altoura and others).

## How you build for ARGO

Two supported paths, both native to the device rather than over a network API:

1. Unity (recommended) — Unity 2022.3.45f1 (some samples 2021.3+), plus the Qualcomm
   Snapdragon Spaces Unity SDK (v1.0.1 / v1.0.0 / v24) obtained from Qualcomm's Snapdragon
   Spaces support team, plus the QCHT hand-tracking package. Build for Android, deploy to
   ARGO.
2. Android Studio — Java/Kotlin against DigiOS head-worn Android APIs and the DigiOS SDK
   (projector/display mode control, IMU sensors, DigiOS-VoiceUI service, native
   stereoscopic 3D). Install with `adb install`.

Each sample declares a minimum DigiOS build (for example B1.0001.2098, B1.0001.2116,
B1.0001.2145) alongside its Unity and Spaces SDK versions — that triple is the real
compatibility contract.
